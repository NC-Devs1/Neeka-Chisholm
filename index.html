I'm Neeka Chisholm — a founder and lead developer who takes full ownership of an idea from concept to market: setting the vision, making every product and design call, and driving execution with the discipline of a much larger team.

With 5+ years building digital products — and 14+ years across a career spanning entrepreneurship, logistics, and public-facing operations — I move fluidly between creative direction and technical execution: UI/UX design, brand identity, and full-stack development in Figma, Adobe Creative Suite, After Effects, HTML/CSS/JavaScript, Python, Node.js, and native iOS via Swift and Xcode.

I'm fluent in modern AI tooling — working daily across multiple large language models and agent ecosystems, Model Context Protocol (MCP) integrations, GitHub version control, and PostgreSQL. Beyond client work, I build owned products that solve real problems: my flagship app Healthy Eat's is live on the Apple App Store. My mission is simple — build digital experiences as unique as the people behind them, beautifully and reliably.
