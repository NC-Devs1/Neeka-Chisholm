About

Building digital
experiences that last

I'm Neeka Chisholm — Founder and Lead Developer at NC-Developer, LLC, a boutique mobile and web development studio that turns bold ideas into powerful digital realities.

With 5+ years of experience, I specialize in taking projects from concept to launch — handling everything from native iOS applications to full-stack web platforms, UI/UX design, and brand identity.

Beyond client work, I build owned products that solve real problems. My flagship app Healthy Eat's is live on the Apple App Store. I believe authentic work creates lasting impact, and my mission is simple: to build digital experiences as unique as the people behind them — beautifully and reliably.
